Setup By Device

Pick your device and follow the numbered steps.

Smart TV

Samsung · LG
  1. Open the app store on your TV and install Smart IPTV or IBO Player.
  2. Note the MAC address shown on the app's start screen.
  3. Send that MAC address to us on WhatsApp.
  4. We activate your device and your channel list loads automatically.

Firestick / Fire TV

Downloader app
  1. Enable Apps from Unknown Sources in Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options.
  2. Install the Downloader app and enter the link we send you.
  3. Install IPTV Smarters Pro or TiviMate.
  4. Log in with the credentials we sent and start watching.

Android TV / Android Box

Google Play
  1. Open Google Play and install IPTV Smarters Pro, TiviMate or XCIPTV.
  2. Choose Login with Xtream Codes API.
  3. Enter your username, password and server URL.
  4. Wait for the channel list to load, then start streaming.

Android Phone / Tablet

Google Play
  1. Install IPTV Smarters Pro from Google Play.
  2. Select Login with Xtream Codes API.
  3. Enter the username, password and server URL from WhatsApp.
  4. Tap Add User and open Live TV, Movies or Series.

iPhone / iPad / Apple TV

App Store
  1. Install IPTV Smarters Player or Flix IPTV from the App Store.
  2. Choose Xtream Codes login (or Add M3U URL).
  3. Enter your credentials exactly as we sent them.
  4. Save and wait for the playlist to load.

MAG Box / Enigma2

Portal / M3U
  1. Go to Settings > System Settings > Servers > Portals on your MAG box.
  2. Enter the portal URL we provide and save.
  3. Restart the device from the menu.
  4. For Enigma2, send us your device details and we'll provide the M3U line.

Troubleshooting Tips

Most issues are solved in under a minute with one of these.

Buffering or freezing

Restart your router, switch to a 5GHz network or an ethernet cable, and close background apps.

Channels not loading

Fully close and reopen the player, or re-enter your credentials — copy and paste rather than typing.

Login failed

Check for extra spaces in the username or password and confirm the server URL is exactly as we sent it.

Wrong channel list

Refresh the playlist inside the app settings; your list updates automatically once refreshed.
